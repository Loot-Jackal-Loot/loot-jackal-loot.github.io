The year is 30XX.

Two strange space-hunters are wandering around infinite space in search of abandoned spacecrafts, full of treasures to resell! Jackal, the arm. Sphyx, the mind.

But there's a twist! Every spacecraft is equipped with a safety system that will make it explode after 30 seconds.

Sphyx wouldn't be able to deactivate it in time!

Steal as much as you can in just 30 SECONDS. LOOT JACKAL LOOT!

Tap around the map to move Jackal, and use your fingers to complete different types of mini-games.

Every mini-game that you complete without failing increases your multiplier and makes you score EVEN MORE POINTS!

Challenge yourself or the entire world to make the best score that the galaxy has ever seen!
